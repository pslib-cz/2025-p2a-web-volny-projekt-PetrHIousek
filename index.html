    FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

    • How long does the delivery take?

      Your server is created automatically right after the payment goes through. In most cases it is running within five minutes and you get the login details by e-mail.

    • Can I upgrade my server later?

      Yes. Memory, disk space and slots can be changed any time and the price is recalculated for the rest of the paid period. The server only restarts once during the change.

    • What happens if I do not pay on time?

      Your server keeps running for three days after the due date. After that it is suspended, but your data stays with us for another fourteen days before it is deleted.

    • Do you offer a refund?

      If a service does not work as promised, write to our support within fourteen days of the order and we will refund the unused part of the period.
